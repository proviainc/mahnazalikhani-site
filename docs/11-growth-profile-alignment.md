Status: Approved source of truth

Scope: `mahnazalikhani.com` public identity, routing, claims, and brand relationships

## Decision

The website remains Mahnaz Alikhani's personal authority and credibility site,
but it does not operate a separate Mahnaz services funnel.

- Public roles: **Founder, ProVia Events** and **Co-Founder, EventAgent.io**.
- Primary commercial route: tracked ProVia Events structured intake.
- LinkedIn: secondary professional connection route.
- Speaking, emcee, event strategy, and event-service inquiries: ProVia Events.
- Hospitality and software history: credibility context, not separate offers.
- EventBoxed: planned as a separate entity; absent from this website for now and
  limited to Mahnaz's LinkedIn About section until its structure and her formal
  role are ready.
- Numeric proof: event counts, audience sizes, adoption totals, percentages, and
  performance claims remain unpublished until current evidence and wording are
  revalidated.

## Attribution

Outbound ProVia Events and EventAgent.io links use stable UTM parameters that
identify `mahnazalikhani.com` as the referral source. Growth owns downstream
source, campaign, signal, relationship, opportunity, and outcome records.

## Authority and safety

Mahnaz approves personal identity, portrait, biography, role dates, and public
claims. ProVia Events owns event-service qualification and delivery after
handoff. EventAgent.io owns product capability and readiness claims. No AI or
automation may publish profile changes or external messages without explicit
approval.

## Supersession rule

This decision and `00-stakeholder-decisions.md` supersede older planning-stage
documents wherever they describe Calendly as the primary personal funnel,
EventAgent as only a strategy-contributor context, hospitality consulting as a
separate offer, or numeric proof as currently approved for public use. Historical
documents remain useful research records but are not current publication
authority on those points.
